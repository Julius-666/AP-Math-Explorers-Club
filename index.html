About

What the club is

The AP Math Explorers Club meets weekly to work through competition problems, explore topics past the AP curriculum, and prepare students for national math competitions. All skill levels are welcome — we pair newer members with experienced solvers.

Meetings mix problem sets, short lessons on techniques that don't show up in class (proof writing, combinatorics tricks, number theory), and open work time before competitions. [Add your meeting time, room, and advisor here.]
